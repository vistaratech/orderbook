About Us (கடை புக் பற்றி)

Making Shop Billing Simple, Fast & Accessible

KadaiBook (kadaibook.in | kadaibook.com) is built with a singular mission: To bring effortless digital order management, GST billing, and credit tracking to every small business owner in Tamil Nadu.

Our Vision (எங்கள் நோக்கம்)

Across Tamil Nadu and India, millions of retail shops, grocery stores (மளிகைக் கடைகள்), textile merchants, and restaurants still struggle with heavy paper notebooks, lost credit records, and expensive, hard-to-use billing software.

KadaiBook provides a 100% cloud-synced digital solution that runs smoothly on any smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer without requiring costly hardware. Shop owners can generate bills in seconds, send WhatsApp receipts, and track every single rupee of customer credit (கடன் கணக்கு) with complete peace of mind.

Who Uses KadaiBook?

KadaiBook is actively used by:

  • Provision & Kirana Stores (மளிகை & பலசரக்கு கடைகள்)
  • Textile, Saree & Garment Shops (துணிக்கடைகள்)
  • Bakeries, Cafes & Small Eateries (பேக்கரி & உணவகங்கள்)
  • Mobile & Electronics Retailers (எலக்ட்ரானிக்ஸ் கடைகள்)
  • Wholesale Distributors & Traders (மொத்த வியாபாரிகள்)
