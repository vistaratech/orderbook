Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Can I use KadaiBook on multiple phones or computers?
Yes! When you log in with your KadaiBook account on other phones, tablets, or desktop browser, all orders, products, customers, and payments sync in real-time instantly.
What happens to my orders & customer records if my subscription ends?
Your data is 100% safe and permanent. You will never lose any past orders, customer ledger entries, or invoices. You can access all existing data anytime on the Free tier.
How do WhatsApp bills and payment reminders work?
With KadaiBook Pro, you can send professional itemized invoices with your store logo and UPI QR code directly to your customer's WhatsApp in 1 tap, and automated reminder alerts for pending balances.
Can I connect Bluetooth Thermal Printers?
Yes! KadaiBook supports all 2-inch and 3-inch standard ESC/POS Bluetooth thermal printers for fast receipt printing at your billing counter.
