How It Works

Three Simple Steps to Collect Dues Faster

1

Add Customer (வாடிக்கையாளர் சேர்ப்பு)

Enter the customer name and phone number once. Their complete credit history will be stored safely on the cloud — accessible from any device.

2

Record Credit or Payment (வரவு / செலவு)

When a customer buys items on credit or pays back money, update the amount in one second. The balance is automatically calculated with full transaction history.

3

Send WhatsApp Reminders (வாட்ஸ்அப் நினைவூட்டல்)

Send a polite, automated WhatsApp reminder along with payment QR codes so customers can pay directly via GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm.
