DATA DELETION REQUEST

Account and Data Deletion

At KadaiBook, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data and business ledger information. If you no longer wish to use KadaiBook, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.

Types of Data Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following information is permanently erased from our active servers within 14 days:

  • Your personal profile (Name, Phone Number, Email Address)
  • Your business profile and settings
  • All ledger entries (Customers, Products, Orders, and Payments)
  • All associated images or attachments you uploaded to your account

How to Request Deletion

To request the deletion of your account and all associated data, you have two simple options:

Option 1: Instant in-app deletion

If you are logged into the KadaiBook mobile application:

  1. Go to Settings screen.
  2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your password to instantly erase all your data.

Option 2: Email request

  1. Send an email to our support team at: info.vistaratech@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. In the body of the email, include the phone number or email address linked to your KadaiBook account for verification.

Data Retention Policy

Once an account deletion request is initiated, your data is immediately queued for permanent erasure. Due to automated backup cycles, it may take up to 30 days for archived data to be completely overwritten, during which it will remain inaccessible.
