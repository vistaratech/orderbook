Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes the policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of your personal and business data when you use our mobile application KadaiBook and our website accessible via kadaibook.in (collectively, the "Platforms"), developed and operated by KadaiBook ("we", "us", or "our").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using KadaiBook. By using or registering on KadaiBook, you agree to the terms described in this Policy and our Terms of Service.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you use KadaiBook, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your business name, owner name, mobile phone number, email address, and store address.
  • Business & Transactional Data: Invoices generated, orders, customer names, contact numbers, credit balance amounts (கடன் கணக்கு), and expense records that you choose to store in the app.
  • Media & Images: Store logos or invoice photo attachments you optionally upload using your camera or gallery with your explicit permission.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash diagnostic logs to ensure app performance and stability.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Providing seamless digital order booking, invoice creation, and billing functionality.
  • Synchronizing your business data securely across your mobile devices via encrypted Google Cloud / Firebase servers.
  • Enabling automated WhatsApp invoice sharing and payment reminder generation for your customers.
  • Providing customer support and troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access to your account.

3. DATA OWNERSHIP & ZERO THIRD-PARTY SELLING

Your business data belongs to you. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your customer lists, sales figures, or business transaction data to third-party marketing companies or data brokers.

4. DATA SECURITY & STORAGE

We implement enterprise-grade security practices:

  • All data transmissions between the KadaiBook app and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud / Firebase) protected by role-based authentication and security rules.
  • Regular security assessments and automated backups prevent accidental data loss.

5. YOUR RIGHTS & ACCOUNT DELETION

You have full control over your data. At any time, you may:

  • View, edit, or update your store and profile information directly in the app.
  • Export your customer records and sales reports to PDF or Excel formats.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated transactional data via the in-app Settings or through our Account Deletion Page.

6. POLICY CHANGES

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
